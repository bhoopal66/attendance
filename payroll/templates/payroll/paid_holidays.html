Each employee receives days × (their gross for the month ÷ days in their pay period). Sundays are excluded from the day count because a Sunday is never deducted for — and the divisor is the full pay period, the same one absence deduction uses, so a day paid and a day deducted are the same size. Anyone with no gross this month (commission-only staff) is skipped rather than paid from a made-up rate.
