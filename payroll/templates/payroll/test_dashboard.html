How Sales Performance — Remote pay is calculated
✕
Live from July 2026 salary onwards. Talktime-proportional daily-pay model ("Method 2") — this is the calculation used for remote Sales: Performance employees in the Payroll Summary (rows there are marked "M2"). Months before July 2026 still used the original present/half/absent calculation for the actual payroll (see the "Sales Perf: Legacy" tab); those months are shown here only for reference. Daily wage = salary ÷ working days in the period (calendar days minus Sundays/holidays); Sundays/holidays themselves are paid in full. Fridays & Saturdays: binary 30-minute threshold — <30 min = absent (0 pay), 30+ min = full day pay (no half/proportional zone). Monday–Thursday, new joiner's first calendar month: <45 min = absent, 45–59 min = half day, 60+ min = full day paid at MAX(daily wage, talktime÷60 × daily wage) — so strong first-month talktime is rewarded above the flat daily wage. Monday–Thursday, standard (month 2+): <45 min = absent, 45–54 min = half day, 90+ min = full day; 55–89 min is a grace band paid proportionally (talktime ÷ 90 × daily wage) — but only if the employee hasn't exhausted their grace days: max 7 grace days per calendar month, max 2 per Mon–Sun week, and max 2 consecutive grace days in a row. A grace-band day that fails any of those caps is downgraded to half day pay instead. Target override (INR only): if an employee's total bank account submissions for the month reach 4, they're paid full salary for the period regardless of attendance (marked "Target Hit"). In-house Sales: Performance employees are not shown here since they have no talktime data — see the "Sales Perf: In-House" tab for those. Note: per-call data (call duration/internal/CRM-disposition filtering) isn't stored in this system, so talktime minutes come directly from the daily call-record total rather than a call-by-call filtered total.
